THE CONNECTION BETWEEN THEM

One history.
Three useful views.

  • History answers “what did I do?” Tap a date to see every session, exercise, and completed set.
  • Summary answers “how have I trained?” Switch week, month, or year; see session types, locations, and activity.
  • Consistency answers “am I finding a rhythm?” Weekly goals reward repeat attendance while leaving room for rest.
  • No invented fitness score. Active days and sessions stay distinct. An unfinished week is still in progress.
Fictional data for visual review. Calendar/history/reporting are not connected to the native app yet.
