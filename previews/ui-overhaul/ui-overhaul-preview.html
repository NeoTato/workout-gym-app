THE PROPOSED DIRECTION

Meaningful color.
Fewer competing buttons.

  • One primary action per screen. Coral leads you forward; secondary actions step back.
  • Positive actions feel different. Green outlines invite adding. Green checks confirm completion.
  • Destructive actions are explicit. Red text and clear labels, with confirmation before removing completed work.
  • Sets become rows, not stacks of controls. Exercise settings tuck behind the menu. Optional exercises can fold away.
Review the direction before implementation. No changes to the Expo app are included in this preview.
